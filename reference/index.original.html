How to read the signals

  • Beta is how far a coin moves for a 1% BTC move (1.5 means about 1.5%). Correlation is how reliably it follows BTC day to day (1 is perfectly). Trust a beta only when its correlation is high.
  • Correlation dropping (15d or 30d amber) means decoupling. The coin is moving more on its own; check “vs BTC” for which way.
  • Low correlation and vs BTC positive: breaking away upward. This is the alt-season pattern, where BTC goes sideways and the coin keeps climbing.
  • Low correlation and vs BTC negative: breaking away downward, from coin-specific weakness or money rotating out.
  • Low correlation and vs BTC near zero: noise so far. Wait for a direction.
  • Correlation still high but 15d beta well above 90d: still following BTC but moving harder, often the first stage of an alt rally.
  • When coins are decoupling, switch the estimate window to 15 or 30 days, since the 90-day beta describes the old phase.
  • Stablecoin dominance rising while BTC is flat means cash is building on the sidelines. Falling faster than BTC explains means cash is being spent into crypto, often alts.

“vs BTC” is the coin’s return minus BTC’s: +12% means it beat BTC by 12 percentage points. The comments use 15-day numbers: a correlation under 0.6 counts as decoupling, and beating or lagging BTC by 5 points or more counts as a direction.

How the page works

  • Saved scans. Opening the page shows the last saved scan and makes no TradingView calls. Refresh pulls 95 daily bars for each coin one at a time, about 1.5 seconds apart (roughly a minute in all), recalculates everything and saves it. A coin that fails keeps its previous result, and Fetch missing coins retries only those.
  • Estimate. Price now × (your BTC price ÷ BTC now)beta, with the beta from the window you pick. Beta and correlation use daily log returns on the days a coin and BTC both traded.
  • Sources. Binance USDT pairs through TradingView, except VVV from Bybit (Binance has no VVV spot history). Stablecoin dominance is CRYPTOCAP:USDT.D + USDC.D and CRYPTOCAP:STABLE.C.D.
  • Before acting, remember these are estimates from past behaviour. Coins break from BTC on their own news. Not financial advice.
